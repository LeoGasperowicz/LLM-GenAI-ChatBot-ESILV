First job survey 2024
Engineers graduating in the class of 2023 (for the 2024 survey) were interviewed 6 months after graduation.
How do ESILV
Master in Engineering
graduates fare in the job market after completing 5 years of engineering studies? This is the question answered each year by the first job survey.
The first job survey is carried out each year by ESILV as part of the survey conducted by the Conférence des Grandes Ecoles, a few months after graduates leave the school.
46 800 €
average gross annual salary
95%
executive status
92%
hired in less than 4 months
6 months after leaving ESILV
Graduates’ situation
89% net employment rate
91% hired on permanent contracts
20% working abroad
92% of contracts signed in less than 4 months
Search time
Nearly 69% of graduates in post found a job before graduating.
92% of graduates in post found their job in less than 4 months
.
Starting salaries
Starting salaries range from €33,000 to €178,000 (gross annual salary, including variable pay).
The average salary is €46,800 in France and €57,000 abroad.
Type of contract
91% of contracts are permanent
85% are satisfied or very satisfied with their first job
International
20% start their career abroad. Of the ESILV class of 2023 who have stayed in France, 44% have an international job.
Company size
4% work in companies with fewer than 10 employees
4% in SMEs with between 10 and 19 employees
8% in SMEs with between 20 and 49 employees
15% in companies with between 50 and 249 employees
33% in companies with between 250 and 5,000 employees
36% in organisations with more than 5,000 employees
