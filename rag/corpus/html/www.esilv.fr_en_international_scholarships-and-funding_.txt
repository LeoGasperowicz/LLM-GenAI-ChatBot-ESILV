Tuition Fees
For the 2026-2027 academic year:
Master in Engineering (Grande Ecole programme)
€11400 per academic year
MSc programmes
€13400 per academic year
with the following options:
Option 1
: additional module of French as a foreign language courses at €2,000
Option 2
: additional full online module of 30 ECTS credits, increasing the programme from 90 to 120 ECTS credits, for an additional tuition fee of €3,500
Early Bird waiver
The Early Bird is a waiver of 20% of the tuition fee for the 2026-27 academic session at EMLV, ESILV or IIM.  The Early Bird waiver is compatible with any other scholarship.
Eligibility
Non-French candidates applying through the
international application portal
for entry to EMLV and ESILV
Application process
The Early Bird waiver is granted automatically if all criteria are met
Receipt of the Early Bird waiver will be confirmed in writing on the enrolment letter (issued after payment of the deposit)
Calendar for applications
All eligible candidates who finalise their enrolment on their chosen programme by making the deposit payment before March 31st will automatically be granted the Early Bird waiver for the ensuing September/October intake
All eligible candidates who finalise their enrolment on their chosen programme by making the deposit payment before October 15th will automatically be granted the Early Bird waiver for the ensuing January intake
Scholarships
De Vinci Excellence Scholarship
De Vinci Excellence Scholarship
Convinced of the immense importance of improving international representation in engineering, business and multimedia, the De Vinci Higher Education Group has made available funds for talented students hoping to pursue their studies at ESILV and EMLV schools.
This scholarship is awarded in the form of a tuition fee waiver for the student’s first or second year of study, worth up to 12900€, awarded by De Vinci Higher Education group. We are sure that the De Vinci Excellence Scholarship will support international students during their studies at ESILV and EMLV. We believe by making education accessible to everyone we will inspire the next generation in pursuing their goals and ambitions.
Eligibility
Non-French candidates applying through the
international application portal
at Master level for September/October intake are eligible for consideration for the scholarship
Candidates applying for ESILV or EMLV must show a GMAT test score of 650 minimum or an equivalent GRE / CAT test score
Candidates must not be over 30 years of age on September 1st 2025
Application process
Candidates must first secure admission to their programme of study and meet the above criteria for eligibility to the scholarship
Selected candidates will be invited to submit a 1000-word essay (topic to be defined in line with the programme of study)
Calendar for scholarship applications
Candidates must submit their programme application before March 2025
The scholarship recipient will be notified in writing in April 2025
Eiffel Excellence Scholarship Programme
Eiffel Excellence Scholarship Programme
The Eiffel Excellence Scholarship Program was established by the French Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs to enable French higher education institutions to attract top foreign students to enroll in their masters and PhD programs.
It gives opportunity to the future foreign decision-makers of the private and public sectors, in priority areas of study, and encourages applicants up to 25 years old from developing countries at master’s level, and applicants up to 30 years old from developing and industrialized countries at PhD level.
For more information, check
https://www.campusfrance.org/en/eiffel-scholarship-program-of-excellence
